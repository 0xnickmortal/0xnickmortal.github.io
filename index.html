Part 107 HQ — FAA Remote Pilot (UAG) Practice & Chart Trainer

Browser-based study platform built around official 14 CFR Part 107 text, FAA Airman Knowledge Testing Supplement figures, and current 56-day VFR sectional chart editions following the October 26, 2026 PSI exam roll-out.

  • Current VFR Sectional Chart Practice Questions — interactive excerpts covering airport data blocks, obstacle elevations, and airspace boundaries.
  • Free 60-Question Timed Part 107 Mock Exam — weighted to the PSI UAG Information Bulletin (48% regulations, 20% airspace, 25% operations, 5% weather, 2% loading).
  • What Changed on the October 2026 Part 107 Exam — breakdown of the FAA September 2026 Special Edition Advisory on non-supplement chart images.
  • How to Read a Sectional Chart for Part 107 — step-by-step symbol guide with worked examples.
  • CTAF vs. UNICOM Frequencies on Sectional Charts — locating circled-C markers and distinguishing tower, ATIS/ASOS, and UNICOM frequencies.
  • AGL vs. MSL Altitude Conversion Guide — tower 400-foot structure rules and terrain elevation math.
  • Part 107 Airspace Classes (B, C, D, E & G) — § 107.41 authorization rules, magenta vignettes, and dashed surface areas.
  • 14 CFR Part 107 Rules Summary With Section Numbers — concise reference to operating limits, night lighting, Remote ID, and Categories 1–4.
